## Takeaways & Reflection from Guest Lecture
+Wednesday’s guest lecture helped me understand how engineering standards support safety, compatibility, and reliability. The idea of standards as shared agreements about design, testing, and communication stood out to me. Having a uniformly aligned reference helps people work together more easily and reduces misunderstandings, which is expected to reduce systemic failures. I enjoy or I could resonate with how the speaker uses all kinds of relatable examples to explain the idea.One of the most memorable example would be the difference in iMessage UI for apple users and none apple users. The lecture also made me reconsider the relationship between standards and reality. While standards provide requirements, engineers still need to consider situations that those requirements may not fully address or being restricted by unexpected issues. I'm able to resonate with this topic more as we work on our labs and the project itself. Although writing comments for lines of codes each time seems really complicated, but the logic behind is to allow others to understand what you're writing even if it's their first time seeing this code.
